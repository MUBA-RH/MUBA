# Continuity Contract

MUBA must continue operating without ChatGPT, its current developer, or any individual operator.

Durable knowledge planes:
1. GitHub: source, architecture, protocols, schemas, migrations, recovery and release manifests.
2. Future persistent operational database: story state, jobs, publications, truth, media, failures and health.
3. Human daily report: what happened, health, what happens next, DEV action required.

Chat memory is not a production database. A replacement AI/developer reads START_HERE, verifies repository reality and asks whether DEV wants current recovery or V2. It must guide a non-specialist DEV one verified step at a time.

Stopping use of ChatGPT must not delete GitHub or independent provider resources. Provider operation still depends on their own accounts, credentials, policies and availability.

Anti-amnesia rule: no critical operating rule may exist only in an AI conversation. Accepted production policy belongs in code/config/docs/tests as appropriate.

# Continuity Contract
MUBA must continue operating without ChatGPT, its current developer, or any individual operator.

Durable planes:
1. GitHub: source, architecture, protocols, schemas/migrations, recovery and releases.
2. Future persistent operational database: truth, story, jobs, publications, media, failures and health.
3. Human daily report: what happened, health, what happens next, DEV action required.

Chat memory is not a production database. No critical production rule may exist only in an AI conversation.

A replacement AI/developer reads START_HERE, verifies repository reality and asks DEV which path is wanted. It must guide a non-specialist DEV one verified step at a time.

Stopping use of ChatGPT must not by itself stop/delete independent GitHub, runtime, Telegram, X, database or other provider resources; those remain subject to their own accounts, credentials, availability and policies.
